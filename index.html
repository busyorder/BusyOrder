Questions people ask before they start

How much does a website or app cost?

Websites start from ₹15,000, Android apps from ₹20,000, UI/UX design from ₹7,000 and college projects from ₹5,000. The final price depends on pages, features and your deadline. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. Send your idea through the quote form and you'll get a timeline together with your price, usually within 24 hours.

Do I need to create an account to order?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Pick a service, fill in your details and pay online, or send an enquiry and get a quote first.

Can you build my college project?

Yes. I build mini and main projects for engineering, IT and computer science students, including AI, blockchain, security and Android projects, with documentation.

Do you work with clients outside Trichy?

Yes. Projects are handled remotely over WhatsApp, email and calls, so you can work with Busy Order from anywhere in India.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

You review the work as it's built and can ask for changes along the way. Refund rules are on the Refund Policy page.
